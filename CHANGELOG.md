# Changelog

- Prevented spells without their own cooldown, such as Fireball, from pulsing after the global cooldown when combat hides timer values.

- Kept global cooldowns out of combat pulses.

## 1.60.1-forever.4 - Forever

- No gameplay changes in this Forever branch; the existing spell, item and pet cooldown flashes remain available.
