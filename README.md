<h1 style="color: #d9a94b;">LH CooldownPulse</h1>

<p>Sometimes you only need a quick glance to know an ability is ready again. LH CooldownPulse flashes its icon near the middle of the screen when a cooldown finishes, then gets out of the way.</p>

<h2 style="color: #73c9c4;">What you can change</h2>

<ul>
  <li>Adjust the icon's size, animation, timing and opacity, and move it with a preview.</li>
  <li>Optionally show spell names, track pet abilities and tint pet cooldown icons.</li>
  <li>Exclude selected spells or items, or use the list as an allowlist instead. The filter is saved per character; animation settings are shared across your account.</li>
</ul>

<h2 style="color: #c78be6;">Set it up</h2>

<p>Open <strong>Options → AddOns → LH Tweaks → LH CooldownPulse</strong> or type <code>/lh_cooldownpulse</code>. Unlock the preview to drag the pulse into place, use <strong>Test</strong> to see the current animation and enter spell or item names or IDs in the filter field.</p>
